# Sprite Room: Agents as Pixel Art
Card: project, right column
Object: a laptop, its deck cut away, the pixel room hanging from its notch
Code-drawn version: `svgs/draw/scenes.py`, `spriteRoom()`

## Claims → details
| Claim (from the description) | Detail on the object |
|---|---|
| A macOS app that drops from the notch | a panel hanging from the camera notch on the screen |
| Each agent a character, each tool call something it visibly does | pixel sprites at desks, one reaching for a shelf |
| Native, SpriteKit and AppKit | the deck cut away to the logic board, fan and battery |

## Signature detail
The room hanging from the notch: that is the app, exactly.

## Prompt
```
Technical engraving-style line illustration, black ink on pure white background, no color, no gray fills, no gradients. Drawn like an engineering patent plate crossed with an exploded-view assembly diagram: precise uniform outlines, finer interior lines, shading only with parallel hatching, cross-hatching and stippling. Isometric three-quarter view from about 30 degrees above. Schematic elements (arrows, connector lines, brackets, dashed guide lines) are drawn as physical engraved lines within the scene. Any code or text is represented only as rows of short horizontal dashes with indentation, never readable characters. Single composition, centered, fully in frame with generous white margin. No labels, no numbers, no letters, no background scenery, no border.
Composition flows toward the lower left.
A modern laptop open at about 100 degrees, three-quarter view: the near corner of its keyboard deck cut away with section-hatched edges, showing the logic board, chips, a cooling fan and battery cells beneath; keys and a trackpad on the rest of the deck, ports on the side. On the screen, a rounded panel hangs down from the inked camera notch holding a pixel-art room: blocky sprites of two characters at desks, one reaching up to a shelf, a tiled floor; dashed code rows below. Avoid: readable text, logos, faces, glow.
```
