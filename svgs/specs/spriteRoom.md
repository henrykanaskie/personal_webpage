Card: project, right column
Object: a laptop, its deck cut away, the pixel room hanging from its notch
Code-drawn version: `svgs/draw/scenes.py`, `spriteRoom()`

## Claims → details
| Claim (from the description) | Detail on the object |
|---|---|
| A macOS app that drops from the notch | a panel hanging from the camera notch on the screen |

## Prompt
```
Highly detailed technical illustration in black ink line art on a pure white background, in the style of a vintage engineering patent drawing and a cutaway plate from a technical manual. Intricate: hundreds of small parts, screws, seams, cables and fine surface detail. Clean confident outlines with thinner interior lines; shading only with fine parallel hatching, cross-hatching and stippling that follows the form. No color, no gray fills, no gradients, no large solid black areas. Three-quarter isometric view from slightly above. Single object, centered, fully in frame with generous white margin. No text, no labels, no numbers, no letters, no background, no ground shadow, no border.
Composition flows toward the lower left.
A modern laptop open at about 100 degrees, three-quarter view: the near corner of its keyboard deck cut away with section-hatched edges, showing the logic board, chips, a cooling fan and battery cells beneath; keys and a trackpad on the rest of the deck, ports on the side. On the screen, a rounded panel hangs down from the densely hatched camera notch holding a pixel-art room: blocky sprites of two characters at desks, one reaching up to a shelf, a tiled floor; dashed code rows below. Avoid: readable text, logos, faces, glow.
```
