# Sprite Room: Agents as Pixel Art
Card: project, right column
Concept: machine: a pixel room hanging from the laptop notch, fed one way by a tape of tool-call events

## Claims → parts
| Claim (from the description) | Part |
|---|---|
| Drops from the notch (macOS app) | laptop lid cutaway, room sliding down out of the notch |
| A live agent's activity as a pixel-art room, each agent a character | isometric pixel-stepped room with one character mid-action |
| Each tool call something it is visibly doing | tape of uniform event blocks entering the room |
| Read-only by design | one-way check valve on the tape, flap open only toward the room |
| Replay harness keeps the scene deterministic | used tape winding onto a reel |

## Signature detail
The one-way check valve: read-only is a deliberate design decision, and it is the part nobody else's drawing would have.

## Cut order
1. replay reel
2. lid cutaway layers
3. extra furniture in the room

## Prompt
```
Technical engraving-style line illustration, black ink on pure white background, no color, no gray fills, no gradients. Drawn like an engineering patent plate crossed with an exploded-view assembly diagram: precise uniform outlines, finer interior lines, shading only with parallel hatching, cross-hatching and stippling. Isometric three-quarter view from about 30 degrees above. Schematic elements (arrows, connector lines, brackets, dashed guide lines) are drawn as physical engraved lines within the scene. Any code or text is represented only as rows of short horizontal dashes with indentation, never readable characters. Single composition, centered, fully in frame with generous white margin. No labels, no numbers, no letters, no background scenery, no border.
Composition flows toward the lower left.
An isometric cutaway of the top edge of a laptop screen, seen from slightly above and in front, the aluminum lid cut away in a clean section to show its thin layered construction. At the center of the top bezel is the camera notch, and hanging down from the notch like a drawer sliding out is a small isometric room built from visible square pixels with stepped staircase edges: a floor of pixel tiles, two walls, a tiny desk with a monitor, a shelf, and one blocky pixel character mid-action reaching toward the shelf. Behind the laptop, entering from the upper back, a flat ribbon of tape carries a sequence of small uniform rectangular blocks, like packets or punched cards, each one a tool-call event; the tape runs down behind the screen and into the back wall of the room. Where the tape enters the room it passes through a small mechanical one-way check valve drawn in cutaway, with its flap hinged open only in the direction of the room, making clear information flows in but nothing flows back. Off to one side, the tape's used end winds onto a small film reel. The laptop and valve are drawn in smooth precise engraving lines; only the room and character are pixel-stepped, so the two materials contrast. Avoid: speech bubbles, game UI, health bars, cute faces, readable screen content, logos.
```
