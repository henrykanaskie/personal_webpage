# GrowthApp
Card: parts list (APP-02), private
Subject: the orb: the app's home screen, a sphere of habit filaments with a node per kept day, ringed by its readouts

Source: `henrykanaskie/growthapp`, README and `App/Views/OrbView.swift`. The home
screen is "the orb": a turning sphere where each habit is a filament radiating from
the centre, every kept day a node on it, nearby nodes stitched into a faint web, the
day just kept glowing gold. It sits on "the board", ringed by readouts (sleep, steps,
heart rate, weight, journal, the habit grid of crosses).

## Claims → details

| The board of readouts around the orb | six small tiles ringing the sphere: heart rate, steps, sleep, weight, journal, grid of crosses |

## Signature detail
The filaments beaded with one node per kept day, today's node ringed: the record growing as you keep it.
