# Monte Carlo Portfolio Risk Engine
Card: project, left column
Concept: machine: a block bootstrap press that cuts joint price history into blocks, shuffles them, and lands the paths in a fat-tailed histogram

## Claims → parts
| Claim (from the description) | Part |
|---|---|
| Correlation from joint historical sampling | one tape carrying several parallel price lines together |
| Block bootstrap | guillotine cutting contiguous blocks with all lines intact, tumbled in a wire drum |
| Reports the distribution of outcomes | fan of stitched simulated paths |
| Measured against a Gaussian baseline | thin dashed bell curve over the histogram |
| Especially the ugly tail | histogram's left tail longer and fatter than the dashed curve, darkest hatching |

## Signature detail
The fat left tail escaping the dashed Gaussian: the tool exists to show that tail.

## Cut order
1. fan of paths (land blocks straight in the histogram)
2. reel at the left
3. drum cutaway detail
